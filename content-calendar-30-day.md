# 30-Day Instagram Content Calendar

**Dates:** Mon Nov 9 – Tue Dec 8, 2026
**Assumed niche:** creators/coaches turning an audience into an owned business (swap in yours).
**Cadence:** 5 feed posts/week (Mon–Fri), light story/engagement days on weekends.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Goal | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Educate | Reach, saves | 40% |
| Proof | Trust | 20% |
| Story | Connection | 20% |
| Offer | Leads/sales | 10% |
| Community | Comments, DMs | 10% |

**Offer posts** use a comment-keyword CTA (e.g. "Comment GUIDE") so you can DM the freebie.
**Hook rule:** put the hook in the first 2 seconds and as on-screen text.

## Workflow
1. **Batch:** film all reels for a week in one 2-hour session on Sunday.
2. **Design:** build carousels in Canva using one brand template.
3. **Schedule:** queue posts in Metricool as drafts and approve before publishing.
4. **Review:** check saves, shares, and DM keyword replies each Friday. Double down on the top 2 formats in the next cycle.

## Placeholders to personalize
- Replace "creators/coaches" and the example hooks with your niche.
- Add your real numbers wherever a Proof post says so. Do not invent results.
- Set your own offer and dates for the Nov 27–29 sales posts.
